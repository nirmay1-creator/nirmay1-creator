### Who Am I?

I am a **B.Tech Cybersecurity** student, currently building full-stack security-focused platforms and preparing them for hackathons and academic submission. My work sits at the intersection of **offensive security**, **digital forensics**, and **product engineering** — I like taking a system apart to understand it, then rebuilding it so it can't be broken the same way twice.

Over time, I've developed hands-on experience across the **web development ecosystem** — from `FastAPI` and `PostgreSQL` backends to `Docker`-orchestrated deployments — with a strong pull toward **cyberpunk-styled, security-first interfaces** that make complex forensic data feel intuitive.

At the moment, I'm deep in **JusticeFlowX**, a digital forensics and cyber-investigation platform, moving it from prototype to a production-grade, containerized architecture.
